# Mentor guide: running the Alpha Academy Trading Floor

A one-page guide for brothers running market sessions with Alpha Academy. No technical setup needed.

## Before the first session

1. Open the site and choose **Mentor panel**.
2. Create a mentor login with your email and a password, then enter the mentor code from the chapter's web team.
3. Choose **New game**. Suggested first-game rules:
   - Starting cash: $100,000
   - Single-stock limit: 25% (funds like SPY are exempt, which nudges students toward diversifying)
   - Lowest share price: $1.00 (keeps penny stocks out)
   - Fractional shares: on
   - Classmates see holdings: on, if you want students to learn from each other's picks
4. Write a short announcement. It shows at the top of every student's home screen.

## At the first session (about 50 minutes)

1. **Join (5 min).** Put the game's QR code on screen. Students scan it, choose a display name (first name and last initial), and make a 4 to 6 digit PIN. No email needed. Names appear on the game's roster within a few seconds, so you can count them against the students in the room to see who has not joined yet.
2. **Learn (15 min).** Everyone reads "Order types" in Learn, watches one of its short videos, and takes the lesson exam: 8 questions on their own screen, passed at 75%.
3. **Research (10 min).** Each student picks a company they know and answers the eight questions in "Researching a company."
4. **Trade (10 min).** Students place their first trade and fill in **Why this trade?** One or two sentences is enough.
5. **Debrief (10 min).** Open two or three students' pages from the roster and talk through their notes. Close on the leaderboard and the S&P 500 line.

## Each week after

- At the start of each session, check the count above the roster ("4 of 5 active now"). It shows at a glance if a student who joined earlier has not opened the app today.
- Assign one lesson and its exam before trading opens. The roster counts lessons passed, and each student's page shows every best score.
- Use the lesson's "Talk it over" questions to open the session. They are written for mentor discussion.
- Ask students to write a note on every trade. Notes are the best window into how they are thinking.
- Use the announcement to set a theme ("This week: diversify across three sectors").
- Look at who is ahead of the S&P 500 benchmark, and ask why most professional funds are not.

## Questions students ask

**"Why did my order not go through?"** Open Orders, then History. Every unfilled order shows the reason, such as not enough buying power or the day order expiring at the close.

**"Why did my price change after I hit buy?"** When the market is closed, market orders wait and fill at the first price after the 9:30 a.m. opening bell. That is how real brokerages work too.

**"I'm on a new phone."** Choose "I already joined" and enter the game code, the same name, and the PIN. If they forgot the PIN, reset it from the roster.

**"Can I use real money?"** Not here. The Learn track "Money for Life" covers custodial accounts and Roth IRAs for when they are ready, with a parent or guardian.

## Mentor tools

- **Pause** stops trading (for example, during a test week). **End** closes the game and keeps the final leaderboard.
- **Reset the game** sends everyone back to starting cash and clears holdings and history. Players and lesson progress stay. Good after a practice round.
- **Last active** on the roster: a green dot means the student has the app open right now (within the last 5 minutes), gold means active in the last hour, and gray means earlier. A student whose phone screen locked or who switched apps turns gold after about 5 minutes.
- **Reset PIN**, **Rename**, and **Remove** are on each roster row.
- **Download CSV** exports the roster for program records.

Play money for learning. Nothing in the app is investment advice.
