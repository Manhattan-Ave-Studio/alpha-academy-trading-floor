# Mentor guide: running the Alpha Academy Trading Floor

A one-page guide for brothers running market sessions with Alpha Academy. No technical setup needed.

## Before the first session

1. Open the site and choose **Mentor panel**.
2. Create a mentor login with your email and a password, then enter the mentor code from the chapter's web team.
3. Choose **New game**. Suggested first-game rules:
   - Starting cash: $100,000
   - Single-stock limit: 25% (funds like SPY are exempt, which nudges students toward diversifying)
   - Lowest share price: $1.00 (keeps penny stocks out)
   - Fractional shares: on
   - Classmates see holdings: on, if you want students to learn from each other's picks
4. On the game's page, under **Required lessons**, check "Order types" and press **Save required lessons**. Students in the game cannot trade until they pass it, so the first lesson comes before the first trade. The roster's Required column shows who is through.
5. Write a short announcement. It shows at the top of every student's home screen.

## At the first session (about 50 minutes)

1. **Join (5 min).** Put the game's QR code on screen. Students scan it, choose a display name (first name and last initial), and make a 4 to 6 digit PIN. No email needed. Names appear on the game's roster within a few seconds, so you can count them against the students in the room to see who has not joined yet.
2. **Learn (15 min).** Everyone reads "Order types" in Learn, watches one of its short videos, and takes the lesson exam: 8 questions on their own screen, passed at 75%. Each exam allows 3 tries, and the best score counts. A student who misses three times sees "No tries left" until you reset the exam from their page (see Mentor tools).
3. **Research (10 min).** Each student picks a company they know and answers the eight questions in "Researching a company."
4. **Trade (10 min).** Trading opens for each student as soon as they pass "Order types". They place their first trade and fill in **Why this trade?** One or two sentences is enough. A student who has not passed yet sees "Trading opens after you pass your required lessons" with a link back to Learn.
5. **Debrief (10 min).** Open two or three students' pages from the roster and talk through their notes. Close on the leaderboard and the S&P 500 line.

## Each week after

- At the start of each session, check the count above the roster ("4 of 5 active now"). It shows at a glance if a student who joined earlier has not opened the app today.
- Assign one lesson and its exam before trading opens: add it under **Required lessons** on the game's page and save. Trading closes for each student until they pass the new lesson, then opens again on its own. The roster's Required column ("2 of 3") shows who is through, and each student's page shows every best score with tries used. Take a lesson off the list if you would rather let students trade while they catch up.
- Use the lesson's "Talk it over" questions to open the session. They are written for mentor discussion.
- Ask students to write a note on every trade. Notes are the best window into how they are thinking.
- Use the announcement to set a theme ("This week: diversify across three sectors").
- Look at who is ahead of the S&P 500 benchmark, and ask why most professional funds are not.

## Questions students ask

**"Why did my order not go through?"** Open Orders, then History. Every unfilled order shows the reason, such as not enough buying power or the day order expiring at the close. If the home screen says "Trading opens after you pass" a lesson, they have a required lesson left.

**"Why did my price change after I hit buy?"** When the market is closed, market orders wait and fill at the first price after the 9:30 a.m. opening bell. That is how real brokerages work too.

**"I'm on a new phone."** Choose "I already joined" and enter the game code, the same name, and the PIN. If they forgot the PIN, reset it from the roster.

**"Can I use real money?"** Not here. The Learn track "Money for Life" covers custodial accounts and Roth IRAs for when they are ready, with a parent or guardian.

**"I'm out of tries on the exam."** Each exam allows 3 tries, and the best score is kept. Open the student's page from the roster, find the lesson (it carries a Locked chip), and press **Reset exam**. They get 3 new tries. Only the game's admins can do this. Before resetting, go over the lesson's takeaways with them.

## Admins

Each game has admins, chosen from the mentors. Whoever creates a game is its first admin.

- Every mentor can open any game and see its roster and every student's page.
- Only the game's admins can change its rules, pause or end it, reset it, assign required lessons, reset a PIN, rename or remove a player, reset an exam, or add and remove admins. Other mentors see those controls disabled with the note "Only admins can change this game. Ask one of them to add you."
- To add an admin: on the game's page, under **Admins**, enter the brother's email and press **Add admin**. They need a mentor login and the mentor code first, and the email must match the one they signed up with.
- **Remove** takes an admin off the list. A game keeps at least one admin, so add another before removing the last one. If every admin's account is gone, the game is open to every mentor, so no game is ever stuck.

## Mentor tools

- **Pause** stops trading (for example, during a test week). **End** closes the game and keeps the final leaderboard.
- **Reset the game** sends everyone back to starting cash and clears holdings and history. Players and lesson progress stay. Good after a practice round.
- **Last active** on the roster: a green dot means the student has the app open right now (within the last 5 minutes), gold means active in the last hour, and gray means earlier. A student whose phone screen locked or who switched apps turns gold after about 5 minutes.
- **Reset PIN**, **Rename**, and **Remove** are on each roster row.
- **Required lessons** on the game's page: check the lessons students must pass before they can trade, by track, then press **Save required lessons**. Markets, portfolio, leaderboard, and Learn stay open to them while they work on it.
- **Required** on the roster shows each student's progress against that list ("2 of 3", or "none" when nothing is required). The CSV includes the counts.
- **Reset exam** is on each student's page, next to any lesson they have tried and not passed. It gives 3 new tries and keeps the best score.
- **Download CSV** exports the roster for program records.

Play money for learning. Nothing in the app is investment advice.
